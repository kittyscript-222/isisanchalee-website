## MODULE 1 — THE ANATOMY OF THE FEAR OF BEING SEEN (Awareness)

**1.1 What the Fear of Visibility Actually Is** (15 min)
- Definition: a learned survival response to the perceived danger of exposure — not a character flaw, not laziness, not lack of discipline
- It's protection, not pathology. You don't need to judge or reject this side of you.
- Everything you desire is on the other side of your willingness to be seen
- Visibility isn't just social media: relationships, intimacy, asking for what you want, taking up space

**1.2 The Disguises: How Fear of Being Seen Shows Up** (20 min)
The fear rarely announces itself. It wears costumes:
- **Procrastination** — "I'll post when…" (delay = protection)
- **Perfectionism** — "the most beautifully wrapped form of self-sabotage"
- **Over-educating / over-preparing** — one more course, one more certification before I'm allowed
- **Fear of judgment & criticism** — pre-living the rejection so you never risk it
- **Self-consciousness** — performing instead of expressing; watching yourself being watched
- **Safe, "trending" content** — hiding in plain sight; visible but not *seen*
- **Playing small** — undercharging, under-claiming, deflecting compliments
- **The visibility hangover** — posting something real, then hiding for a week
- Teaching frame: *If visibility = danger, then ______ = protection.* (Have them fill in their own blank.)

**1.3 Why Strategy Can't Fix It** (10 min)
- You already know what to post. Information was never the problem.
- Strategy applied on top of a dysregulated nervous system produces contracted, "icky" content — and your audience feels it
- You will be most successful when it's enjoyable and natural — the felt-safe version of you is the magnetic version
- You will keep sabotaging your social media success as long as you take these feelings and emotions as truth

**1.4 The Cost of Hiding / The Ripple of Being Seen** (10 min)
- What hiding has already cost (gently — awareness, not shame)
- The ripple: surface area of visibility → opportunities → authentic relationships → intimacy → self-respect
- Understanding this is protection helps you release self-judgment — this lesson ends with a compassion reframe

**2.1 What Subconscious Beliefs Are & How They Form** (20 min)

**2.3 The Chain: Beliefs → Thoughts → Emotions → Actions → Results** (15 min)

## MODULE 3 — YOUR NERVOUS SYSTEM & POLYVAGAL THEORY (The Body)

**3.1 Nervous System 101** (15 min)
- What the autonomic nervous system is and its one job: keep you alive
- **Sympathetic** (fight/flight — mobilization) vs. **parasympathetic** (rest/digest — restoration)
- The body is constantly scanning for danger, beneath conscious awareness
- The tiger: 200,000 years ago, tiger = death = instant sympathetic activation. The hardware hasn't changed — only the tigers have.

**3.2 Polyvagal Theory: Your Three States** (25–30 min — the anchor lesson)
- **Ventral vagal** (safe & connected): creative, playful, present, connected — where authentic content comes from
- **Sympathetic** (fight/flight): anxious posting, obsessive checking, defensive energy, over-preparing (flight in disguise)
- **Dorsal vagal** (freeze/shutdown): numb scrolling, "I don't even care anymore," ghosting your own account, collapse
- **Neuroception:** your body decides "safe or unsafe" *before* your thinking mind gets a vote
- Map each visibility disguise from 1.2 onto a state (procrastination = freeze/flight; perfectionism = sympathetic; numbness = dorsal)
- The hierarchy: you don't jump from shutdown to safe — you move through activation. Normalize the messy middle.

**3.3 Your Brain Under Threat** (15 min)
- When dysregulated: amygdala activity increases, prefrontal cortex activity decreases — which is why ALL your fears come forward at once when you're activated
- This is why you can't think your way out of a triggered state — the thinking equipment is literally offline
- The nervous system doesn't know the difference between real danger and perceived danger. Thinking about stepping forward, being seen, taking up space → body responds as if genuinely under threat
- If the subconscious belief is "I am not safe when I am seen," your body interprets posting as a real threat to your life — sympathetic response fires. It filters everything through the lens of what you've learned is safe.
- **The rule: never make a decision from a dysregulated state.** Regulate first, then decide. When you return to parasympathetic, the prefrontal cortex comes back online and a different choice becomes available.

**3.4 Learning YOUR States: The Personal State Map** (15 min + worksheet)
- What each state feels like in *your* body (thoughts, sensations, urges, posture)
- Your early-warning signs (jaw, chest, stomach, scrolling, snacking, sudden cleaning)
- Your triggers ladder: which visibility actions activate you at 2/10 vs 9/10
- Window of tolerance: the goal isn't to never leave it — it's to widen it and return faster

**3.5 The Regulation Toolkit** (20–25 min — demonstrate each)
Downshift tools (sympathetic → ventral):
- Physiological sigh (double inhale, long exhale)
- Extended exhale breathing / pranayama
- Orienting: slowly look around the room, name what you see — shows the body there's no tiger
- Grounding: feet on floor, nature, bare feet on earth
- Warm foods, warm drink, warmth on the chest
- Moving slow on purpose; walking at half speed
- Havening / tapping on the chest
- Co-regulation: voice note a safe friend, be around regulated people
Upshift tools (dorsal → mobilized): movement, shaking, music, cold water on face, humming/singing (vagal tone)
- Build your personal **Regulation Menu**: 3 tools for activation, 3 for shutdown, 1 do-anywhere-in-public

**Practice:** Daily state check-in (3x/day, 30 seconds: "What state am I in? What do I need?")
**Audio:** Nervous System Regulation hypnosis — use before any visibility action this week

## MODULE 4 — REPATTERNING: COMPASSION, SHADOW WORK & SELF-TRUST (The Repair)

**4.1 Meeting Your Protector** (15 min)
- Reframe: every disguise from Module 1 is a protective mechanism your brain built to keep you safe. It worked. That's why it's still here.
- How do you feel about the side effects of your self-sabotage? Meet even THAT with compassion — it was survival.
- What your mind needs to know is: *you are safe, and you've got yourself.*
- The protector doesn't need to be defeated — it needs to be reassured. It shrinks when it trusts the adult you.

**4.2 Shadow Work: Finding the Blocks** (15 min)
- What shadow work is (simply): meeting the parts of you that learned to hide
- How to use the Self-Inquiry Journal safely: regulated body first, curiosity not judgment, stop if flooded
- The projection mirror: who do you judge for being "too visible / too much / attention-seeking"? That judgment marks your own cage.
- We are meaning-making machines: take out the meaning, take out the judgment, view the pattern, clean it out. If a whisper says you can't — it's a story, not truth.

**4.3 Self-Trust: The Antidote** (20 min)
- Lack of self-trust makes the inner protector BIGGER; self-trust is what finally lets it stand down
- Years of outsourcing decisions, worth, and intuition to other people taught your nervous system that you can't be trusted
- Inventory: When you don't act from self-trust, where do you betray yourself? (saying yes when you mean no, abandoning your plans, breaking promises to yourself, asking everyone's opinion first)
- What beliefs/stories do you need to let go of in order to trust yourself? Write down the exact internal dialogue that runs when you feel it.
- Rebuilding: small kept promises → evidence → inner authority → empowerment. Self-trust is built transactionally, one kept promise at a time.

**4.4 Rewriting the Story** (15 min)
- Take the core belief from Module 2 and formally rewrite it (e.g., "When I am seen, I am rejected" → "When I am seen, the right people find me, and I can handle the rest")
- The new belief must feel *reachable*, not delusional — the nervous system rejects lies
- Repetition + emotion + regulated body = the same recipe that installed the old belief installs the new one (this is what the hypnosis audios do at night)

**4.5 The Anchor Practice** (8 min)
- Tap on the chest: *"Even though it sometimes feels difficult, I am choosing to accept myself, to trust myself, and to know that I am enough."*
- When to use: before posting, after criticism, during the visibility hangover
- Stack it: regulation tool → chest tap → then the visibility action

**Practice:** 21-day hypnosis protocol (nightly audio) + one micro-promise kept to yourself daily
**Journal:** Shadow Work Journal, Parts 3 & 4 (Protector Dialogue + Self-Trust Inventory)

## MODULE 6 — VISIBLE IN THE REAL WORLD (Integration)

**6.1 The Visibility Ladder** (20 min)
- Graduated exposure with a regulated body — the clinical gold standard, made practical
- Build a personal 10-rung ladder from 2/10 activation (comment on someone's post as your full self) to 9/10 (go live, launch, raise prices)
- Rule: regulate → act → regulate → integrate. Stay at a rung until it's boring, then climb. Boredom = the nervous system saying "this is safe now."

**6.2 The Posting Protocol** (15 min)
- Before: state check → regulation tool → 2 min embodiment → post from ventral
- After: close the app. Do NOT refresh. Regulation practice. The urge to check obsessively is sympathetic energy looking for safety in numbers.
- The vulnerability hangover: normal, temporal, survivable — have a plan for it (warmth, co-regulation, evidence journal), don't let it write the story

**6.3 Consumption Boundaries** (10 min)
- The less you consume, the more you can show up — consumption fills the channel expression needs
- Comparison-scrolling is a dysregulation ritual disguised as "research"
- Set boundaries: creation before consumption; time-boxed scrolling; curate the feed to nervous-system-safe accounts
- Just practice and put in the work — it gets easier. Focus on the next step in front of you.

**6.4 Content That Feels Like You** (15–20 min — bridge to the IG Mastery guide)
- Now that the inside is repatterned, strategy finally works. The three pillars your profile must answer:
  1. **Who I am**
  2. **How to work with me**
  3. **Results / testimonials & case studies**
- Content types: educational (of the build), behind-the-scenes, credibility content
- Think about your ideal client and how *she* would respond — create for her nervous system, from yours
- Hand-off: "Now open the IG Mastery guide — you're finally in the state where strategy sticks"

**6.5 The Final Boss** (10 min)
- Right before the breakthrough, the fears and limiting beliefs have a final cry and get LOUDER — like a video game final boss
- It's not a sign you're off-path; it's a sign you're at the gate. It's just your brain and body trying to keep you safe one more time.
- Protocol: name it ("final boss"), regulate, step away from the belief, take action from the version of you that doesn't have it, look for proof you are that woman

**6.6 Riding the Wave of Success** (15 min)
- Success is also visibility — being received, praised, watched, AND criticized all require capacity
- Condition the subconscious and nervous system to lean in and ride the wave when it comes, instead of flinching, deflecting, or sabotaging it back to baseline
- Receiving practices: let a compliment land for 5 full seconds; celebrate wins somatically; expand ON success, not just on failure

**6.7 Close: Your 30-Day Integration Plan** (10 min)
- Re-take the Module 0 baseline — compare scores and rewrite your visibility story
- The 30-day plan: daily (state check, audio, evidence log), weekly (ladder rung, journal session), monthly (re-assess, new rung)
- Ask what the fear is. Regulate — nature, grounding, warm foods, pranayama, moving slow. Then act from her.
- Invitation to next step (RTT session / custom audio / 1:1) — warm, not pushy
